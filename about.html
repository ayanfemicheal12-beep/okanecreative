About Michael Okanlawon

I am Michael Okanlawon, the founder of Okane Creative. I help authors put their books in front of the readers who would love them.

I started in 2024 and have worked with more than 100 authors since, across research, Amazon pages, outreach, promotion, email and design. Every book and every author’s goals are different, so I begin by studying the book, its category and its readers before I recommend anything.

I am careful about what I promise. I will be transparent about what I do and what comes of it, with a written report every ten days. I do not promise sales, because nobody honestly can. What I do promise is to do everything in my power to make them happen.

The name Okane comes from my surname, Okanlawon. The logo, a quill feather passing through the letter O, stands for what the work is about: books, and the people who write them.
