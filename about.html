About Okane Creative

Okane Creative is a small studio run by Michael Okanlawon. We work with authors one at a time, in plain language, and we say what is working and what is not.

The name comes from the founder’s surname, Okanlawon. The logo, a quill feather passing through the letter O, stands for what the studio is built around: books, and the people who write them.

We work with independent and traditionally published authors, including children’s authors, and we work with clients in different countries.
