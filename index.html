What we do for authors

Marketing research and analysis

Category, keyword, competitor and price research, delivered as a written report with the actions we recommend.

Amazon listings and A+ Content

New descriptions, categories, series set-up and extra page sections that keep readers on your book’s page.

Pitching and outreach

Your pitch, written and sent to podcasts, bloggers, reviewers and reading communities, with every reply reported.

Social media and paid ads

Planned posting, plus Amazon, Facebook and Instagram ads that start small and grow only when sales follow.

Design

Covers, banners, trailers, landing pages and author websites that look like one book, wherever readers meet it.
