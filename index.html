What we do for authors

Amazon listings

New book descriptions, category research and series set-up, so the right readers find your book and the page makes sense when they arrive.

Podcast and newsletter outreach

We find shows, newsletters and reviewers whose audience matches your book, write personal pitches, and follow up politely.

Promotional design

Social cards, banners and book mockups built around your cover and illustrations.

Author websites

Multi-page sites for your books, your story and your contact details.
