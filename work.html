Hector and Heloise in Paris: promotional graphics

A picture book for ages 2 to 12 about two hedgehog siblings, a secret package and the Louvre. It won first place in the Firebird Award for Children’s Education and Picture Book.

What we made. Five social media cards. Each carries one idea, so a parent scrolling past gets one clear reason to look at the book: the story hook, the virtual Louvre tour built into the book, the story behind it, who it suits, and the award.

How it was done. Each card is built around one of the book’s watercolour illustrations, with a short headline and a few lines of text that read comfortably on a phone, in the book’s own colours of cream, navy and red. Click any card to enlarge it.

Watercolour illustrations by Barb Ruble, used here with her permission. Layout and design by Okane Creative.
