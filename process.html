How we work

A simple sequence, so you always know what is happening and what we need from you.

  1. We start with your book

    We look at your Amazon page and your current marketing, and ask who you most want to reach and what you have already tried.

  2. We tell you where we would start

    A short plan: what we will do first, what we need from you, and what we would leave alone for now.

  3. We do the work

    Listing text, outreach, design or a website, depending on what you chose. You approve anything that goes out under your name.

  4. You get a report every ten days

    What we did, what happened, and what comes next. If results are slow, the report says so plainly.

  5. We review together

    After the first month we decide together what to keep, change or stop.

What we will not do

  • Promise sales, rankings or bestseller status.
  • Buy or invent reviews.
  • Post your book in places where it is not welcome.

Book marketing takes time, and results build slowly. We would rather tell you that now than after you have paid for it.

Tell us about your book.

Send the Amazon link and a few lines about who you want to reach. We will tell you honestly where we would start.
