Research and strategy

Know where your book fits before you spend time or money on it.

Marketing research and analysis

The starting point for most projects

A good launch starts with knowing where your book fits and who is already reading books like it. We study your category, your competitors and your readers before anything is written or spent.

  • Category and keyword research: the Amazon categories and search phrases readers actually use to find books like yours.
  • Competitor title breakdown: how the leading books in your category present their covers, descriptions, series and reviews, and what you can learn from them.
  • Price-point analysis: where comparable books sit and what that suggests for your price and promotions.
  • Reader-persona notes: who is likely to buy, where they spend time online and what makes them pick a book up.
  • A written report with the specific actions we recommend for your launch or relaunch.

Author pitch marketing

Your pitch, written and sent

We write your professional pitch, then put it to work. We pitch you to podcasts, book bloggers, reviewers and reading communities, and report back on every placement.

  • Your pitch in three lengths: a one-line hook, a short pitch and a long pitch.
  • Research into the podcasts, bloggers, reviewers and communities whose audience fits your book.
  • Personal pitches and polite follow-ups, sent for you.
  • A report on who we approached, who replied and what came of it.

Hosts and editors decide what they feature, so we cannot promise a placement. We do promise to tell you honestly what happened.

Your book’s home online

The places readers land when they go looking for you.

Amazon listing optimisation

Where most sales are decided

Your Amazon page is where most buying decisions are made. We rewrite it for the readers who search for books like yours, so the right people find it and the page convinces them.

  • A new book description, formatted to be easy to read on Amazon.
  • Category research, plus the request to Amazon to add the best-fitting categories.
  • Keyword and search-phrase review.
  • Series set-up, so each book leads readers to the next.
  • Ready-to-paste text and step-by-step instructions for your KDP account.

Amazon A+ Content

The extra section below your description

A+ Content adds extra sections below the book description: the story, your series, your biography. It keeps readers on the page longer and answers their questions before they ask.

  • A module plan for each book, suited to your genre.
  • Headlines and body text, ready to paste.
  • A series comparison chart for authors with more than one book.
  • An author section with your biography.
  • Image modules designed to match your cover and illustrations.
  • Clear instructions for adding it in KDP, because the screens are fiddly.

Amazon reviews A+ Content before it goes live, which can take up to a week.

Social media promotion and management

A steady, planned presence

Regular posting that keeps your book in front of readers, instead of a burst at launch followed by silence. We plan it, create it, publish it and report on it.

  • A content calendar built around your launch dates and your genre.
  • Cover-led creative: quote cards, graphics and short videos built around your cover and illustrations.

Paid ads: Amazon, Facebook, Instagram and more

Fast to start, easy to waste

Paid ads can put your book in front of readers quickly, and can waste money just as quickly. We start small, learn what works, and increase spend only when sales follow.

  • Audience and keyword research.
  • Ad copy and creative variations, tested against each other.
  • Campaign set-up on Amazon Ads, Facebook and Instagram, and other social platforms.
  • Daily monitoring.
  • Spend scaled only when results justify it.
  • Plain-English reporting on spend, clicks, sales and what we would change.
