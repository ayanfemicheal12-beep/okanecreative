Services for authors

Pick one thing or combine several. Every project starts with a short conversation about your book and what you have already tried.

Amazon listing improvements

We rewrite your book description for the readers who search for books like yours, research better categories, and set up your series so each book leads to the next.

You receive ready-to-paste text and step-by-step instructions for your KDP account, or we talk you through it.

Podcast, newsletter and reviewer outreach

We look for podcasts, newsletters, blogs and reviewers whose audience matches your book, write a personal pitch for each, and follow up.

You see who we approached and what they said.

Promotional design

Social media cards, banners and book mockups, built around your cover and illustrations and sized for the platforms you use.

Author websites

A multi-page website for your books, your biography and your contact details, set up so search engines can read it.

Amazon and Facebook ads

Campaign set-up, monitoring and a plain-English account of what the ad budget did.

Reports every ten days

A written report covering what we did, what happened, and what comes next. It also says so when nothing has happened yet.

Not sure what you need? Send your book’s Amazon link and we will tell you where we would start.
